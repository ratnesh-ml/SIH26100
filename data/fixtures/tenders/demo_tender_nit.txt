2. BIDDER ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (BEC) & STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS:

Clause 1.1 - Legal Identity & Mandatory Registrations:
The bidder must possess a valid Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) under the GST Act 2017 and a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) issued by the Income Tax Department. Bidder must submit copy of Form GST REG-06 and PAN Card. The PAN embedded in GSTIN must exactly match the PAN card.

Clause 2.1 - Earnest Money Deposit (EMD):
Bidder shall submit Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) of Rs. 9,00,000/- (Rupees Nine Lakhs Only) in the form of Bank Guarantee or Demand Draft.
Exemption: Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) registered under Udyam Registration are exempted from payment of EMD in terms of Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012, provided they submit a valid Udyam Registration Certificate in Micro or Small enterprise category. Medium enterprises are not eligible for EMD exemption.

Clause 3.1 - Annual Financial Turnover:
The minimum average annual turnover of the bidder during the last three financial years (FY 2022-23, 2023-24, and 2024-25) shall not be less than Rs. 1,35,00,000/- (30% of estimated tender value). Bidder shall furnish CA Turnover Certificate containing a valid 18-digit ICAI UDIN along with audited financial statements.

Clause 3.2 - Net Worth Solvency:
The net worth of the bidder as per the latest audited balance sheet (FY 2024-25) must be positive.

Clause 4.1 - OEM Manufacturer Authorization:
In case the bidder is not the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), the bidder must submit a manufacturer authorization certificate strictly as per Annexure-I issued by the pump OEM, explicitly authorizing the bidder to quote against Tender No. CPCL/PROC/2026/G-8821.

Clause 4.2 - Technical Experience & Past Performance:
The bidder or their OEM must have successfully supplied at least 1 similar order of high-pressure process pumps of minimum value Rs. 1,50,00,000/- in the preceding 7 years to any PSU, refinery, or petrochemical plant. Bidder shall furnish completion certificates and copy of work order.

Clause 5.1 - Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order (PPP-MII):
Only Class-I Local Suppliers with local content of not less than 50% shall be eligible for purchase preference. Bidder must submit self-declaration indicating percentage of local content. If tender value exceeds Rs. 10 Crores, certificate from statutory auditor is mandatory.

Clause 5.2 - Land Border Sharing Restrictions (Rule 144(xi)):
Bidder must submit a signed declaration regarding compliance with Rule 144(xi) of General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 regarding restrictions on procurement from countries sharing a land border with India.

Clause 6.1 - Validity Constraints & Commercial Conditions:
Bid validity must remain firm for a minimum of 120 days from the bid opening date. All submitted licenses, authorizations, and statutory registrations must be valid as on the bid submission due date.
